## Exercise ##
I enjoy olympic weightlifting in my garage gym. Recently, I also picked up endurance training through running and biking. I completed my first half marathon in October, 2023. Here are some helpful links that have helped me with developing my technique and have influenced my training philosophy.

* [Wil Fleming on Velocity-based training](https://www.amazon.com/Velocity-Based-Training-Weightlifting-Concepts-Applications/dp/1941549543)
* [Gordo Byrn](https://twitter.com/feelthebyrn1?ref_src=twsrc%5Egoogle%7Ctwcamp%5Eserp%7Ctwgr%5Eauthor) has been very helpful in shaping my endurance training [philosophy](https://feelthebyrn.substack.com/p/building-your-long-run).
*	[Mobility 101](http://www.allthingsgym.com/mobility101/). Most of these videos are by [Kelly Starrett](http://www.mobilitywod.com/about/kellystarrett/). These help keep me going even with all the aches and pains.
*	Dan John on [front squats and clean and jerks](http://danjohn.net/2009/12/the-front-squat/)
*	[Articles](http://www.ironmind.com:8080/ironmind/opencms/Lifts/index.html) and [videos](http://www.allthingsgym.com/jim-schmitz-olympic-weightlifting-coaching-tips/) by Jim Schmitz
*	[A Training Program for Beginners](http://www.pendlay.com/A-Training-System-for-Beginning-Olympic-Weightlifters_df_90.html) by Glenn Pendlay
